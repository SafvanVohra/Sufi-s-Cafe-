Welcome to Sufis Cafe (Sufis De Cafe) —

To create a space that feels like HOME

A place where unforgettable stories are shared over steaming mugs, refreshing beverages, and hearty meals. Located in the immediate vicinity of Dawat Restaurant on the Kheda highway, Sufis Cafe has become a favorite destination for highway travellers, families, and food enthusiasts.

Where Comfort Meets Flavour

Situated nearby the Coca-Cola Plant and opposite Sumar Logistics in Kajipura, Kheda, Sufis Cafe is a warm and welcoming highway hideaway where modern café charm meets soulful hospitality.

Right on the bustling Kheda-Ahmedabad Highway, we’ve grown into more than just a café — we’ve become a cherished gathering place for travellers, families, students, and friends.

Our Philosophy

We believe in relaxed living, real conversations, and comfort food that feeds the soul.
