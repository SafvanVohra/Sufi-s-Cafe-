Where Highway Journeys Meet Great Taste...


Step inside and you’ll find yourself wrapped in a welcoming space — rich aromas of freshly brewed coffee, sizzling specialties, and comfortable seating that invites you to relax. Everything about Sufis Cafe is designed to feel like a delightful pause in the middle of your journey — a place where you can sip slowly, eat happily, and reconnect.

Our Roots

Located right alongside the renowned Dawat Restaurant on the Kheda-Ahmedabad Highway (near Coca-Cola Plant, opposite Sumar Logistics, Kajipura), Sufis Cafe (Sufis De Cafe) brings together great coffee, appetizing flavors, and heartfelt hospitality. Our menu is crafted for travelers and food lovers alike — featuring hearty portions, satisfying meals, and refreshing drinks that keep you coming back.

A Space That Belongs to You

We’re proud to welcome highway travellers, local foodies, families, and groups of friends. From road-trippers taking a rejuvenating break to families enjoying an evening meal together, Sufis Cafe belongs to everyone who walks through our doors.

Whether you’re stopping by for artisanal coffee, crispy bites, or sharing celebratory meals with loved ones — our café is your space to feel right at home.

Come as you are. Leave a little fuller — in heart, in spirit, and in appetite.
